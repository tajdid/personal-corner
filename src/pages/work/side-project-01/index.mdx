<Section title="About this project">

Frame by frame is a concept for a small, personal film journal. The idea is a quiet place to keep a watchlist, jot down a few lines after a film and save the scenes worth remembering, without the ratings, feeds and social pressure that come with larger platforms.

The concept treats each film as an entry rather than a score. A note might be a single sentence or a short paragraph, and a scene could be saved with a timestamp and a line about why it stood out.

The main design question is how little the journal can ask of someone while still being worth returning to months later.

</Section>

<Section title="What I explored">

- Keeping note-taking fast enough to happen straight after a film.
- Organising a watchlist by mood or occasion rather than by genre alone.
- Presenting saved scenes so a journal reads more like a scrapbook than a database.

</Section>

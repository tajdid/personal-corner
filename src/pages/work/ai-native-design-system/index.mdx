<Section title="Context">

As more squads contributed to the product, maintaining consistency became increasingly difficult. Components had evolved organically, documentation was fragmented, and emerging AI-assisted workflows highlighted new challenges that traditional design systems weren’t built to support.

Rather than waiting for a formal initiative, I explored how our design system could become the foundation for faster, more scalable product development, both for people and for AI.

</Section>

<Section title="Approach">

### Taking the initiative

This wasn’t part of my roadmap or day-to-day responsibilities. I identified the opportunity, developed the proposal independently and presented it to the wider Product & Technology organisation to build alignment around a longer-term vision. The work covered:

- auditing the existing Stake design system
- identifying scalability challenges
- defining an AI-ready design system vision
- exploring variables, tokens and component architecture
- creating AI-readable documentation conventions
- presenting the proposal to Product & Tech to gain buy-in

### What an AI-ready system looks like

Rather than treating the design system as a collection of reusable components, I proposed treating it as a shared source of truth that designers, engineers and AI tools could all understand:

- **Structured:** consistent naming conventions and information architecture.
- **Token-first:** variables and design tokens as the foundation.
- **Documented:** documentation written for both humans and AI.
- **Composable:** reusable components with predictable behaviour.
- **Connected:** a shared language across design, engineering and product.

</Section>

<Section title="Outcome">

Although this began as a self-initiated exploration, it sparked wider conversations across Product & Technology about how our design system could evolve for AI-assisted product development.

- **Vision established:** a long-term vision for the evolution of the design system.
- **Alignment built:** presenting the vision to Product & Technology built shared understanding and buy-in.
- **Conversation shifted:** the discussion moved beyond UI components towards documentation, governance and scalability.
- **Foundations laid:** patterns around tokens, naming and documentation for future AI-assisted workflows.

</Section>

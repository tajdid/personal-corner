<Section title="About this project">

Backlog is a concept for a lightweight game library. It sorts games into three simple states: up next, in progress and finished. The aim is to make a growing pile of unplayed games feel manageable rather than overwhelming.

Rather than tracking every statistic, the concept focuses on the decision people face most often: what to play next. Short notes on where a game was left off could make it easier to pick something back up after a break.

</Section>

<Section title="What I explored">

- Reducing a library to three clear states without losing useful detail.
- Making it easy to resume a game after weeks away.
- Helping choose the next game based on time and energy available.

</Section>

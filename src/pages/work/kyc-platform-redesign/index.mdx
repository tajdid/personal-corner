<Section title="Context">

Altares wanted to modernise indueD, a due-diligence platform used by compliance teams. I helped rethink the end-to-end experience, simplifying a complex interface and creating a clearer foundation for future development.

The platform had been built primarily around functionality, resulting in a dense interface, inconsistent patterns and limited understanding of what users actually needed. The redesign had to fit a four-week timeframe.

</Section>

<Section title="Approach">

### Research and synthesis

I interviewed five users, audited the existing experience and carried out heuristic and competitor analysis. From there I mapped pain points and user needs, prioritised the highest-value opportunities and defined the journeys we needed to redesign.

### Simplifying without losing depth

Working within the four-week timeframe, I translated the research into a new information architecture, a new visual language and a set of high-fidelity product experiences. What changed:

- simplified portfolio management
- introduced global search
- improved filtering and information hierarchy
- reduced unnecessary interface complexity
- more consistent interaction and visual patterns

</Section>

<Section title="Outcome">

In four weeks, we moved from an ageing, developer-led interface to a redesigned end-to-end concept that gave Altares a clearer direction for future product iterations.

- **More intuitive:** users found the simplified experience easier to understand and navigate.
- **More modern:** testing showed a strong preference for the new visual direction.
- **Built to evolve:** the redesign established a foundation for subsequent product development.

</Section>

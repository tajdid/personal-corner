<Section title="Context">

Stake had built its core proposition around fractional real estate investing. StakeOne explored a different opportunity: giving higher-value investors a way to discover and fully own properties digitally, through the same ecosystem.

The challenge was making a much larger financial commitment feel as simple, clear and trustworthy as the existing Stake experience. StakeOne also began without an established brand direction.

</Section>

<Section title="Approach">

### Giving a new product its own identity

Alongside shaping the product experience, I independently explored how the proposition could feel more premium and distinctive while remaining recognisably connected to Stake. The central idea was simple: one whole property rather than a fraction of one.

I explored:

- StakeOne naming and logo treatment
- visual identity and art direction
- the relationship between Stake and StakeOne
- how the proposition appeared in the product
- waitlist, website and marketing applications
- investor and stakeholder presentation materials

### From discovery to full property ownership

Once the proposition began taking shape, I worked through how StakeOne should live inside the existing Stake app. The challenge was introducing an entirely new ownership model without making it feel like a separate or unfamiliar product. The core journey runs from discovery, through a project detail page, to checkout.

</Section>

<Section title="Outcome">

StakeOne moved from an early proposition into a tangible product and brand direction that teams across the business could understand, discuss and build around.

- **Product defined:** a clear mobile direction for discovering, evaluating and progressing towards full-property ownership.
- **Brand established:** the initial identity and visual language that helped StakeOne feel distinct while staying connected to Stake.
- **Alignment built:** the product and brand artefacts created a shared direction across Product, Engineering, Marketing, Operations, Sales and leadership.
- **MVP progressed:** the direction gained positive stakeholder support and helped move StakeOne from concept towards MVP development and launch.

</Section>
